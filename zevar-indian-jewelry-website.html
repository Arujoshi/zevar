Our craft

From sketch to stone-set, by hand

Every Zevar piece passes through four pairs of hands before it reaches yours.

01

Sketch

Each design starts as a pencil sketch, drawing from temple motifs and old family jewellery.

02

Hand-set kundan

Stones are set one at a time into a lac base, the traditional kundan-kari way.

03

Meenakari

Enamel colour is painted and fired into the metal, layer by layer, by our karigars.

04

Polish and string

The finished piece is hand-polished, and pearls or beads are strung to complete it.
